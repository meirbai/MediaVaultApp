Sound Vault - provides a seamless experience with your favourite media. You can search, save, and play music, videos, books without losing access to it.

Functionality list for this app:
- Search for all media/specific kind of media
- Ability to browse all/type of media
- Music/video player
- Book reader with some customisation

Planned future content plan:
- Download and save offline music/video from various platforms
- Online search/browse of media from free websites
- More customization for book reader
